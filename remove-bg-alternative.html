Where DropBG is honest about its limits

The first image on a new device is slow, because your browser has to download the model once (roughly 40 to 90 MB). After that it is cached and most photos finish in one to three seconds on a laptop.

Quality is excellent on people, products, pets and anything with a clear edge. Like every automatic remover, including remove.bg, the hard cases are fine hair against a busy background, semi-transparent glass and very thin structures such as wires or fences. You get the full-resolution PNG, so touching up an edge in any editor is straightforward.
