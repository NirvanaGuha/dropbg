Questions

Is DropBG really free?

Yes. No credits, no watermark, no daily cap, no account, and no ads. The free remover runs on your own device, so giving it away costs us nothing. An optional one-time Pro unlock pays for the site.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. The AI model downloads to your browser once and every image is processed on your own device. Open your browser's network tab while you use it: you will see the model download and nothing else.

Why is the first image slow?

The first run downloads the model, roughly 40 to 90 MB depending on your device. Your browser caches it, so the next images take a second or two. On a phone or a slow connection the first load can take a minute.

How good is the cutout?

Very good on people, products, pets and objects with a clear edge. Like every automatic tool, it is weakest on fine hair against a busy background, semi-transparent glass and thin wires. You get the full-resolution result to touch up if needed.

Is this a replacement for remove.bg?

For the everyday case, yes: one photo in, one transparent PNG out. Read the full comparison for what changes when remove.bg moves to Canva and what DropBG does differently.

What is Pro Mode, and when is it coming?

Pro is a planned one-time purchase, not a subscription. It will add batch download as a .zip, HD Refine (a second pass with a larger server-side model for hair and fine edges), background presets and a commercial licence with priority support. The free remover stays unlimited and full resolution. Join the list to hear when it launches and lock in the launch price.

Can I use the results commercially?

Yes. The output is your image. There is no licence on cutouts you make here.
